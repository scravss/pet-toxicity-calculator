How the Hydration Calculator works

🧮
Base formula. Metabolic water requirement = Weight(kg)⁰·⁷⁵ × 70 ml/day. This allometric formula is the veterinary gold standard for estimating baseline hydration needs.
🏃
Activity multiplier. Low activity = ×0.85 | Normal = ×1.0 | High = ×1.25. Active pets lose more water through panting, sweating (paw pads), and respiration.
🥫
Diet adjustment. Wet food contains ~70–80% moisture. A pet on a wet-food-only diet can get up to 60% of their daily water needs from food, so their drinking requirement is reduced accordingly.
🥛
Glass reference. We use 250 ml as one standard glass — a widely accepted reference volume. The result shows both ml and glasses for practical use.
