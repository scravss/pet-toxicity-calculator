How the calculator works

🐾
Pet type matters. Dogs and cats have different typical lifespans and cost profiles. Select yours to see tailored hints.
💵
Initial costs are one-time expenses — adoption or purchase fee, vaccines, microchip, spay/neuter, and starter equipment. Typically $500–$2,500.
📅
Annual costs repeat every year — food, insurance, routine vet care, grooming, toys and treats. Dogs average $1,200–$3,000/year; cats $700–$2,000/year.
🧮
Formula: Total = Initial Costs + (Annual Costs × Expected Lifespan). The monthly figure divides this by total months of ownership.
